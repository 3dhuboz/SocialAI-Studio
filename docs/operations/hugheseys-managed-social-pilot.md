# Hugheseys Que managed social pilot

## Proposed outcome

Run Hugheseys social media through the existing SocialAI Studio with Steve managing the content workflow and Macca retaining final control of offers, stock, dates, prices, and publishing approval.

## 30-day pilot

- Up to 16 Facebook and Instagram posts for the month.
- One weekly batch of four Drafts, prepared from current menu items, real photos, events, delivery updates, and specials supplied by Macca.
- Steve checks every caption, price, date, location, link, and image before sending the batch for approval.
- Macca gives one written approval or sends one consolidated change list.
- Approved posts are changed from Draft to Scheduled. Unapproved posts remain Draft and cannot publish.
- A short month-end summary covers posts delivered, reach, engagement, clicks, and customer enquiries where the platforms provide those figures.

## Weekly workflow

1. Macca sends the coming week’s confirmed specials, availability, service locations, dates, prices, and usable photos.
2. SocialAI Studio prepares the content batch as Drafts.
3. Steve reviews the batch for brand fit and factual accuracy.
4. Macca approves the batch in writing.
5. Steve schedules the approved posts.
6. The publisher sends each Scheduled post at its approved time and records the result.

## Safety controls

- The workspace has one visible **Pause automation** control.
- Generated batches always enter the calendar as Draft.
- Drafts cannot be claimed by the publisher.
- Resuming automation is blocked while an overdue or undated Scheduled post exists.
- Old queued records can be returned to Draft for review while the workspace is paused.
- Macca keeps ownership of the Facebook Page and Instagram account. Access uses platform roles and two-factor authentication; no shared personal password is required.

## Setup before the pilot starts

1. Leave Hugheseys automation paused.
2. Move the two old Scheduled records back to Draft and review or discard them.
3. Confirm the current Facebook Postproxy placement still targets the Hugheseys Que Page.
4. Reconnect Instagram through Macca’s Meta login and confirm the selected business account.
5. Agree the weekly information deadline and the person who can approve posts.
6. Prepare the first four-post batch and obtain written approval before resuming automation.

## Macca supplies

- Confirmed prices, dates, service areas, stock limits, and offer conditions.
- Real photos or approval to use a generated image.
- Written approval before new offers or factual claims are scheduled.
- Prompt notice when an approved offer changes or sells out.

## Managed service boundary

The pilot covers content planning, caption writing, image preparation, review, scheduling, and a short monthly report. Paid advertising, continuous inbox monitoring, customer service, photography, video shoots, and website or app changes are scoped separately.

## Start decision

The system remains paused until Macca confirms that he wants the pilot, reconnects Instagram, and approves the first batch. This prevents a discussion about the service from becoming publishing consent.
